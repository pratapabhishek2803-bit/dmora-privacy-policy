Who operates DMora

DMora is operated by Abhishek Pratap Singh. It is currently a prototype for creators to organize supported Instagram conversations, prepare suggested replies, and track collaboration leads. Features and hosting may change as the prototype develops.

Information collected

When you register or use the prototype, DMora may store:

  • Your name, email address, and a password hash used to sign in.
  • Instagram account identifiers and username, authorization status, and a protected access token when you connect an eligible account.
  • Messages received through enabled Instagram features, conversation identifiers, timestamps, read status, labels, and priority.
  • Replies and AI draft text, draft status, the model identifier, and basic review activity.
  • Brand-lead notes, follow-up stages, creator tone settings, and FAQ information you enter.
  • Technical information processed by the hosting provider to deliver and protect the service, such as request time, IP address, and browser details.

Some features are optional. Demo conversations are fictional. The prototype does not import your historical Instagram inbox.

How information is used

Information is used to sign you in; display and organize conversations; prepare replies you choose to request; track leads; maintain workspace security; record basic service activity; and respond to privacy or support requests.

DMora does not automatically send AI-generated replies. You must review and approve a reply before DMora attempts to send it.

When information is shared

DMora shares information with service providers only when needed to deliver an enabled feature:

  • Meta / Instagram: When you connect Instagram, Meta handles its authorization and the messaging features you use. Messages and replies are sent through Meta's APIs.
  • OpenAI: AI processing is optional and off by default. If enabled, and you turn on AI consent and request a draft, relevant recent conversation text and your FAQs are sent to OpenAI to generate that draft. The prototype requests that the API not store the response. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy for its handling of API data.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: They process information needed to run the website, database, and security controls.

DMora does not sell personal information or include advertising trackers in this policy page. Providers may process information in other locations under their own terms and privacy notices.

Storage and retention

DMora retains messages, drafts, webhook records, and activity history for up to 30 days by default. Unresolved send attempts are retained until they are checked. Account details and collaboration lead notes remain while the workspace is active, unless you delete them sooner.

In Settings, you can export your workspace, disconnect Instagram, or delete the workspace. Disconnecting removes the access token stored by DMora; you can also revoke the app through Instagram. Deleting your workspace removes its active DMora records. A deletion request can also be sent to the contact below. Data held by Meta, OpenAI, or other providers is subject to their separate processes and policies.

Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to connect Instagram or use AI drafts. AI requests are sent only when AI is enabled, you give consent in Settings, and you ask for a draft. You can withdraw that consent in Settings.

You may contact DMora to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account. Please do not email passwords, access tokens, or message contents unless they are necessary to identify your request.

Session cookies are used to keep you signed in. The policy page itself does not set advertising cookies.

Security

DMora uses password hashing, workspace access checks, anti-forgery safeguards, and server-side protection for Instagram tokens. No online service can promise perfect security. Please use a unique password, protect your account, and report suspected unauthorized access using the contact details below.

Children

DMora is a creator-workspace tool and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and request its removal.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as DMora changes. The latest effective date will be shown at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected in the policy before they apply to new use of the service.
